One day I was using my staubsauger to clean up after my German shephard.
She had eaten my gebaeck which I was having for breakfast!
I tried to get it back and tripped over the Staubsauger, turning it on.
I fell back into the music player and Rammstein started blasting over the speaker.
I almost choked on my broetchen.
Startled by the music, my Hund chased the Staubsauger into a Biergarten, where a waiter tried to stop it with a giant Broetchen.
But the Staubsauger did not like gebaeck, and fled from the waiter.
I started my pursuit of my Hund with my drahtesel.
While on pursuit, I crashed my drahtesel into Professor Mehlhase vehicle.
The waiter slipped on a pile of SauerKraut and landed in a Porsche, accidentally honking along to the music.
Professor Mehlhase's mehlhase flew out of her hand!


At the baeckerei, a self proclaimed Pantoffelheld demanded a refund because his Apgelkuchen had diappeared, while crumbs fell from his moustache.
